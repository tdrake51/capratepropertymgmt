Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: July 2026

Caprate Property Management ("Caprate," "we," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or interact with our Facebook page, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Our website is primarily informational. We do not currently use contact forms, and we do not ask you to create an account or submit personal information directly on the site.

We may receive information in these ways:

  • When you contact us. If you call or email us using the phone number or email address on our site, we receive the information you choose to share — such as your name, phone number, email, and details about your housing needs.
  • Automatically, through tracking tools. Like many websites, we use tools that collect certain technical and usage information automatically when you visit (see "Cookies and tracking" below).

Cookies and tracking (including the Facebook Pixel)

Our website uses the Facebook Pixel, a tool provided by Meta (Facebook). The Pixel uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your visit — such as pages viewed and actions taken — and helps us understand how people find and use our site, and reach people with relevant information on Facebook and Instagram.

This means Meta may receive information about your visit to our site and may use it in accordance with its own policies. We encourage you to review Meta's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle data.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage how Meta uses your information for ads through your Facebook ad settings. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies; doing so may affect how some websites function.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries about available units and answer your questions.
  • Provide information about housing that may fit your needs.
  • Understand how our website is used, so we can improve it.
  • Reach people who may be interested in our available housing through Facebook and Instagram.

We use the information you share when you contact us for the purpose of responding to you. We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. With tools and services that help us operate, such as Meta (for the Facebook Pixel and advertising) and our website host.
  • When required by law. If we are legally required to disclose information, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to contact us through the site, and you can decline to share information you'd prefer to keep private.
  • You can control cookies and tracking through your browser and through your Facebook/Meta ad settings, as described above.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information — such as the right to access or request deletion of it. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Fair housing

Caprate Property Management is committed to equal housing opportunity. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, source of income, or any other characteristic protected by federal, Maryland, or Baltimore City law.

Children's privacy

Our website is intended for adults seeking housing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
