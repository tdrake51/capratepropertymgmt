The process

Straightforward, from first call to keys.

No runaround and no surprise fees. Here's how a placement actually goes with Caprate.

Step 01

Reach out

Call, email, or refer a client. Tell us the voucher type and bedroom size, and we'll point you to units that fit.

Step 02

Tour & apply

See the home in person or virtually. We submit the Request for Tenancy Approval and coordinate the inspection promptly.

Step 03

Move in

Once approved, we finalize the lease and hand over keys — and stay responsive after move-in, too.
