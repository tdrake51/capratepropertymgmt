About Caprate

Housing that's ready, and people who answer.

Caprate Property Management operates renovated residential rentals across Northwest, Northeast, and Central Baltimore. Every home we list accepts Housing Choice Vouchers and HUD-VASH.

Most of our work happens alongside case managers and placing agencies, which shapes how we run: units are finished before they're advertised, paperwork moves quickly, and there's one person on the other end of the phone rather than a queue. When a client is ready to move, the unit should be ready too.
